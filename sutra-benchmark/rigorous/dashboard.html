Caveats

  • n = 3 per cell — exposes the (large) variance but not significance; every cost/turn arm-comparison range overlaps. A firm claim needs ≥10 trials/cell.
  • DS2 gold is ambiguous (dify has several valid "stop a long run" mechanisms); its raw scores are excluded from the quality read.
  • DL3 is partly out-of-scope (answer in the external graphon package) — depresses dify-lexical scores for all arms and made the index-only constraint unenforceable there (a real index-vs-filesystem limitation).
  • Semantic strength is asymmetric: frappe tickets are strongly semantic (concept unnamed in code); dify names its concepts, so its semantic tickets are weaker. Flagged per ticket.
  • Single blind grader per answer; read-only tracing tasks only.
